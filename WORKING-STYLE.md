# Working Style and Development Methodology

## Overview

My working style is centred on independent thinking, logical reasoning, experimentation, and continuous improvement.

I prefer understanding problems before attempting to solve them. My approach is to analyse requirements, identify relevant patterns, explore possible solutions, and translate ideas into practical implementations.

I generally prefer working independently and managing development activities at my own pace.

## 1. Problem-Solving Approach

I approach technical problems by first trying to understand their underlying structure.

My general process involves:

1. Identifying the problem and its requirements.
2. Breaking complex problems into smaller components.
3. Analysing relationships, patterns, and dependencies.
4. Exploring possible approaches.
5. Selecting a practical solution.
6. Implementing and testing the solution.
7. Reviewing the result and identifying improvements.

I value understanding why a solution works rather than simply accepting an implementation without examining it.

## 2. Logical and Analytical Thinking

Logical reasoning is an important part of how I approach technical work.

I am particularly interested in:

- Recognising patterns and relationships.
- Understanding system behaviour.
- Analysing cause-and-effect relationships.
- Identifying inconsistencies and potential problems.
- Exploring alternative solutions.
- Understanding the reasoning behind technical decisions.

Mathematics is one of my favourite subjects because of its emphasis on logic, structure, and analytical problem-solving.

## 3. Independent Working

I generally prefer independent work.

Working independently allows me to:

- Explore ideas without unnecessary interruptions.
- Concentrate on complex problems.
- Organise tasks according to my own approach.
- Experiment with different solutions.
- Maintain control over development decisions.

I value clear requirements, structured workflows, and the ability to investigate problems independently.

## 4. Practical Learning

I prefer connecting technical learning with practical implementation.

Rather than limiting learning to theoretical explanations, I aim to understand concepts through experimentation and application.

My learning process generally includes:

1. Exploring an unfamiliar concept.
2. Understanding its purpose and fundamental principles.
3. Examining practical examples.
4. Experimenting with implementations.
5. Observing behaviour and results.
6. Identifying mistakes and correcting them.
7. Applying the knowledge to practical development.

I consider practical experimentation an important part of developing technical competence.

## 5. AI-Assisted Development Workflow

AI assistance is an important component of my current development process.

I use OpenAI ChatGPT as my primary AI learning and development assistant.

My typical AI-assisted workflow includes:

1. Identifying an idea or problem.
2. Defining the intended functionality.
3. Discussing technical approaches and possible architectures.
4. Exploring implementation options with AI assistance.
5. Reviewing and integrating generated code.
6. Testing the implementation.
7. Investigating errors and refining the solution.
8. Improving documentation and project structure.

I consider AI-generated code an implementation resource that requires review, testing, and understanding.

The purpose of using AI is to accelerate exploration and implementation while progressively improving my own technical knowledge.

## 6. Development and Experimentation

I enjoy exploring ideas and converting them into practical software.

My development interests include command-line utilities, automation, developer tools, and experimental applications.

I value:

- Clearly defined functionality.
- Practical usefulness.
- Understandable implementation.
- Predictable behaviour.
- Appropriate error handling.
- Testing and verification.
- Maintainable project structures.

I prefer developing software incrementally rather than introducing unnecessary complexity at the beginning.

## 7. Debugging and Troubleshooting

I consider debugging an essential part of software development.

My preferred troubleshooting process is:

1. Reproduce the problem.
2. Identify the relevant component.
3. Examine error messages and available information.
4. Investigate the underlying cause.
5. Evaluate possible corrections.
6. Apply a targeted fix.
7. Test the correction.
8. Check that existing functionality remains intact.

I aim to understand the cause of an error rather than relying exclusively on superficial fixes.

## 8. Project Organisation

I prefer projects to have a clear and understandable structure.

My organisational priorities include:

- Meaningful file and directory names.
- Clear documentation.
- Logical separation of responsibilities.
- Consistent coding practices.
- Version control.
- Reproducible testing procedures.
- Maintainable project structures.

I use GitHub to maintain repositories and track development changes.

## 9. Continuous Improvement

I consider software development an iterative process.

An initial implementation is a starting point rather than necessarily the final result.

I aim to improve my work through:

- Testing and debugging.
- Code review.
- Documentation improvements.
- Better error handling.
- Simpler implementations where appropriate.
- Stronger technical understanding.
- Gradual expansion of functionality.

## 10. Current Working Environment

My current development workflow operates primarily through an Android smartphone and Termux.

This environment requires practical adaptation because it does not provide all the conveniences of a traditional desktop development setup.

I therefore focus on using available tools effectively and maintaining organised development workflows.

Acquiring a suitable computer is a future objective that will allow me to expand my development capabilities.

## Closing Perspective

My approach to development is based on independent reasoning, practical experimentation, and continuous learning.

I aim to understand the systems I work with, develop solutions to meaningful problems, and improve the quality of my work through consistent practice.
